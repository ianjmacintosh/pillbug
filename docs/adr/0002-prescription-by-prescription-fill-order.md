# Prescription-by-prescription order for Fill Sessions

When guiding a Patient through filling their Pill Organizer, the app works through one Prescription at a time across all Compartments, then moves to the next Prescription. The alternative — Compartment-by-compartment (fill Monday AM completely, then Tuesday AM, etc.) — feels more intuitive because it mirrors the physical layout of the organizer, but it requires multiple bottles to be open simultaneously. Keeping one bottle open at a time reduces the risk of placing the wrong pill in a Compartment, which is the primary source of medication error during manual organizer filling. The safety benefit outweighs the slight mismatch with the organizer's physical layout.

Note: this workflow is provisional pending validation by a pharmacist or occupational therapist.
